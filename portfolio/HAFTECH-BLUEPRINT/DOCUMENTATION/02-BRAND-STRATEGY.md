# 02 — Brand Strategy

**Objective.** Give HafTech a positioning that is specific enough to guide copy, design and project selection.
**Everything below is `PROPOSED` and requires approval.** It contains no claims about clients, results or awards.

## 2.1 Brand purpose
To turn ambitious ideas into well-engineered software that holds up after launch.

## 2.2 Mission
Design, build and ship digital products and systems end to end, from first idea through architecture, development and deployment, with one accountable engineering team.

## 2.3 Vision
A studio recognised for the quality of its engineering decisions as much as the quality of its interfaces.

## 2.4 Core values
| Value | What it means in practice | Visible in the site as |
|---|---|---|
| Engineering first | Structure before surface | Architecture diagrams as primary visuals |
| Clarity | Say what is true, plainly | Short sentences, no buzzwords |
| Craft | Details are decisions | Precise grids, typography, motion |
| Honesty | Never overstate | Status labels and attribution on every project |
| Ownership | Take responsibility for outcomes | Named roles, explicit contribution matrix |

## 2.5 Personality
Direct · intelligent · composed · mature · human · technically informed. Not loud, not futuristic, not salesy.

## 2.6 Brand promise
You will understand what is being built, why it is built that way, and who built it.

## 2.7 Audiences
| Audience | Primary question | What proves it |
|---|---|---|
| Founders and small-business owners | "Can this studio take my idea all the way to a working product?" | Process page, product stories, clear contact path |
| Product and operations leads | "Will the system be maintainable and integrate with what we have?" | Architecture explorer, case-study technical sections |
| Collaborators and technical peers | "Is the engineering real?" | Founder page, journal, code and architecture illustrations |
| Hiring managers (secondary) | "What has this person built and how do they think?" | `/founder` |

## 2.8 Differentiation
1. **Engineering is shown, not just asserted**: interactive architecture and request-lifecycle visualizations.
2. **Transparent attribution**: every project states ownership, status and who did what.
3. **Founder-led and accountable**, without making the studio depend on one person's biography.
4. **Editorial restraint**: a calm, architectural interface instead of the agency-template look.

## 2.9 Positioning statement
For ambitious businesses and founders who need software built properly, HafTech is an independent engineering studio that designs and delivers digital products, systems and automation end to end, because good products depend on the systems behind the interface.

## 2.10 Communication style
- Lead with the point. One idea per sentence.
- Prefer concrete nouns (API, schema, deployment) to abstractions ("solutions", "synergy").
- State status honestly: "In development", "Concept", "Live".
- Use "we" for the studio and "I" only on `/founder`.
- Never use: best, leading, world-class, cutting-edge, revolutionary, award-winning, trusted by hundreds. Do not state numbers that cannot be sourced.

## 2.11 Brand vocabulary
**Use:** engineer, architect, system, build, ship, structure, layer, interface, infrastructure, product, decision.
**Avoid:** solutions, leverage, disrupt, next-gen, seamless, game-changing, synergy, "digital transformation".
